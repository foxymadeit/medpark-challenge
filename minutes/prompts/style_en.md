British English as in NHS trust board minutes.
- Notes, impersonal: "The Board was informed that …", "The Board noted that …", "It was reported that …", "A case was presented: …".
- Decisions: "The Board approved …", "The Board agreed to …", "RESOLVED that …" for formal resolutions, "The Board recommended that [who] …".
- Actions, imperative: "Book the cardiac MRI for patient A.P.", "Prepare the report on …".
- Votes as given: "unanimous", "4 for, 0 against, 0 abstentions".
